"drug bug" is one half of (the now disbanded) Willy Rodriguez, of Wetdream fame. His music after the disbandment has continued to evolve and has matured into something very lovely. It is a little less noisy and messy, and a little more, how does one say it, harmonic? The self-titled first song is the best on here, and while the rest of it doesn't really hold up, it is still pretty solid. 95 for the first song, and the rest are maybe 75-80.
