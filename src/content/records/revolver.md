I never really got Abbey Road, which was the first Beatles record I listened to. Since I am from a forsaken, young generation, I did not have the joy of either listening to, or being alive when the Beatles were incredibly prominent. I tried to understand them in Abbey Road, and I couldn't (as a friend would accuse me of, I am whimsical enough for it), for the most part (except Octopus's Garden, I love that song), but I figured I might try Revolver, at the very least, since many recommended it as an introduction to their discography.

As it turns out, this was a great decision. It is a fairly short album, with pretty short songs, but I have found all of them to be great! The first half of the album is an incredible run, and I can definitely see what the charm of this album is. I need to do a more in depth listen of it, though, before I do a proper rating. Wonderful, anyways.
