A very good, underrated album. It is a tragic album, considering what samlrc went through, but it ultimately helped her move out and well, you can read the context behind it on albumoftheyear. Musically, it has some great instrumental solos, and although it doesn't have much lyrical content, I still think most of it is quite poignant. It does appeal to me quite a lot, samlrc being trans-fem and this album tackling themes of that, so I might be a little biased, but many of the instrumentals here echo "The Glow Pt.2", for example. So if you're a fan of that, try it out. It is pretty good.
